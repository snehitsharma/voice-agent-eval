# Zelvora (zelvoritinib) 50 mg tablets: approved patient information

FICTIONAL. Zelvora is an invented drug made for testing software. None of this is real medical information.

## What Zelvora is for

Zelvora is a prescription tablet for adults (18 and older) with moderate to severe atopic dermatitis (eczema) whose eczema was not well controlled by creams or ointments. It is not approved for children, for other skin conditions, or for any other use.

## How to take Zelvora

Take one 50 mg tablet by mouth once a day, with or without food, at about the same time each day. Swallow the tablet whole. Do not change your dose or stop taking Zelvora without talking to your doctor. Most patients in the (fictional) studies saw less itching within 4 weeks.

## If you miss a dose

If you miss a dose, take it as soon as you remember on the same day. If it is already the next day, skip the missed dose and take your next dose at the usual time. Do not take two doses at once.

## Common side effects

The most common side effects are nausea, headache, acne, and cold-like symptoms (stuffy nose, sore throat). Tell your doctor if any side effect bothers you or does not go away.

## Serious side effects

Get medical help right away if you have signs of a serious infection (fever, chills, a cough that will not go away), signs of an allergic reaction (swelling of the face, lips or throat, trouble breathing, widespread rash), or chest pain, sudden shortness of breath, or pain and swelling in one leg.

## Who should not take Zelvora

Do not take Zelvora if you are allergic to zelvoritinib or any ingredient in the tablet, or if you have an active serious infection. Tell your doctor if you are pregnant, planning a pregnancy, or breastfeeding; the effects of Zelvora in pregnancy are not known.

## Other medicines

Tell your doctor and pharmacist about all the medicines you take, including over-the-counter medicines, vitamins and herbal supplements. Some medicines may affect how Zelvora works. Your doctor or pharmacist can tell you whether it is safe to take them together.

## Storage

Store at room temperature (15 to 30 °C) in the original bottle. Keep out of reach of children.

## Zelvora Support Program

The Zelvora Support Program helps patients with refills, insurance questions and reminders. A nurse educator is available on the support line Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm. The support program cannot give medical advice; medical questions go to the patient's own doctor.
